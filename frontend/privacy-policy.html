Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information ShopSphere collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using ShopSphere you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Order information: the products you buy, shipping addresses, and payment method (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Usage data: basic information needed to operate the store, such as your cart and order history.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate sign-in.
  • To process orders, show order status, and manage inventory.
  • To send transactional emails such as email verification and password-reset codes.

Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as your theme. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Security

Passwords are hashed with BCrypt and access is protected by short-lived tokens. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, though no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can review and update your profile and addresses at any time from My Profile. To request deletion of your account or data, contact us below.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@shopsphere.com.
