Help Center

Find quick answers to the most common questions about shopping on ShopSphere.

Ordering

Browse the catalog, open a product, choose a quantity and add it to your cart. When you're ready, open your cart and proceed to checkout. You'll need to be signed in to place an order.

Payments

At checkout you can choose Card, UPI, Net Banking, or Cash on Delivery. Card, UPI and Net Banking orders are confirmed immediately; Cash on Delivery is collected when your order is delivered.

Order status & tracking

Open My Orders to see every order and its current status (Placed, Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled). Status updates appear in real time while the page is open — no refresh needed.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order yourself within 24 hours of placing it, as long as it hasn't shipped, from the My Orders page. Stock is returned automatically when an order is cancelled.

Your account

  • Update your name and profile photo, and manage shipping addresses, from My Profile.
  • Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the sign-in page.
  • You can sign in with email and password, or with your Google account.

Still need help?

Email us at support@shopsphere.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
